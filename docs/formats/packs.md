# Resource packs

The graphics a mission loads ([ADR 0004](../adr/0004-resource-packs.md)). Since [ADR 0018](../adr/0018-original-data-at-run-time.md) the runtime packs a mission's GRPs into pages when it starts (`kp_pack_build`, `native/kp_present/kp_packs.c`, stage D5) by the rules below, and reads the sounds and the music from their WAVs and Oggs through `kp_res` (the music streamed). Since stage D8d the converter writes no `PACK` or `MUSC` container, and since [ADR 0019](../adr/0019-install-or-repack-converter-in-c.md)'s stage C2 no `MSND` (a language's speech, now read from its own files: [the repack](repack.md)). Every container below is a [Koprulu container](container.md).

- **Pack membership:** `converter/packs.py`, compiled into the runtime (`tools/defs_c.py`). GRP geometry, atlas names per GRP and each GRP's first frame within its atlas: [`grpmeta.kpd`](kpd.md#grpmetakpd-schema-1)'s layout, which `kp_grpmeta_from_files` builds.
- **Packer:** `kp_packs.c`; its reference is `converter/emit/pages.py` (`tests/c/test_present.c` places frames as it does).
- **Dump:** `python3 -m converter dump FILE`.

All integers are little-endian.

## The packs' pages

**Frames.** The frames of an atlas are those of the grpmeta `GRPS` entries whose atlas it is, in `GRPS` order, each GRP's frames 0 … n − 1 in order. Frame f of `GRPS` entry e is the record `first(atlas of e) + AFRM[e] + f` of the entry's pack (grpmeta `AFRM`). A record's width and height are the GRP frame's; the frame's texels are rows `y … y + height − 1`, columns `x … x + width − 1` of its page, row-major, top row first. A frame of zero width or height is the record (0, 0, 0, 0, 0) and takes no page space. Frames with identical pixels (same width, height and indices) share one rectangle: stock GRPs repeat many frames, so the six packs store 10,654 distinct images for 20,598 frames.

**Pages.** Every page of every pack is 2048 × 2048, so the runtime can put the pages of all loaded packs into one texture array. A texel is the palette index, 0 transparent; every texel outside the frames is 0. The runtime reads texels by integer coordinates, so frames have no padding, extrusion, rotation or trimming. Pack pages have no opacity mask: a GRP that draws an opaque index 0 is refused (`OpaqueZeroError`; ADR 0003; no stock GRP does). Portrait atlases are not part of the packs.

**Packer** (skyline, `SKY1` version 1). The distinct frame images are sorted by height, then width (both descending), then by first use. Each goes to the first page where it fits, at the skyline position with the lowest top edge, ties to the lowest x; an image that fits no page opens a new one. The same inputs give the same pages.

## HD art (ADR 0013): `HDPK` (schema 3) and `HDTS` (schema 2)

StarCraft: Remastered's HD world (`hd2`: ×2, `hd`: ×4) comes from the player's install or from the repack ([ADR 0019](../adr/0019-install-or-repack-converter-in-c.md), stage C5). From an install the runtime lays the packs out from the anims at a mission's start and builds their pages when it needs them (`kp_present/kp_anim.c`), and builds the tileset's and the water's containers (`native/kp_app/kp_hd_scr.c`); `kp_convert repack` writes the same containers byte for byte into the HD sets' groups ([the repack](repack.md)), `python3 -m converter hd` wrote them into `generated/hd/<variant>/` until then. Either way the runtime reads them with `kp_present/kp_hd.c` (the containers) and `kp_hd_render.c` (the frame table, the terrain atlas) and draws them with `kp_gfx`'s HD world (`--graphics`). The interface's `ui.kpd` comes the same two ways since stage C6: laid out from the install by the placement table, or the repack's (below).

**`HDPK`** (the repack's `koprulu/<set>/packs/<pack>.kpd`), one per SD pack, its frames in the SD pack's `FRMS` order (so an SD frame's global index is its HD frame's):

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| `HEAD` | u8 scale (2 or 4), u16 page size (2048), u16 pages, u32 frames, u8 the pages' format (0: BC3 diffuse and BC1 team blocks; 1: ETC2 RGBA8 with EAC alpha and EAC R11, the same sizes; 2: ASTC 4 x 4 and 8 x 8; the lit layers in the phones' section below), u16 lit pages (the first pages) |
| `FRMS` | per frame: u16 page, x, y, width, height of the HD frame in its page; i16 dx, dy: its centre less the SD frame's centre times the scale, in half HD pixels, y down (both boxes are centred on the image's position); width 0 without HD art |
| `DIFF` | the pages' BC3 blocks: the diffuse sheets |
| `TEAM` | the pages' BC1 blocks: the team colour masks (zero where an image has none) |
| `NRML` | the lit pages' BC3 blocks: the normal maps (x in red, y in alpha); empty without lit pages, as the three below |
| `AODP` | their BC3 blocks: the ambient occlusion (green) and the depth (alpha) |
| `SPEC` | their BC1 blocks: the specular colours |
| `EMIS` | their BC1 blocks: the emissive colours (black where an image has none) |

An image's frames come from its own anim (`anim/main_<image>.anim`, `HD2/anim/…`) or the one `SD/images.rel` names; a GRP takes the first images.dat entry naming it. Each frame keeps its BC blocks: its rectangle in the sheet widened to whole blocks (a rectangle two frames share is placed once; its frame's `x`, `y` are inside it). The rectangles are packed by image, tallest first, bottom-left along a skyline into the last four pages still open, else a new page, so an image's frames lie on few pages. The images Remastered lights (their anim has `normal` and `ao_depth`) are packed first, onto pages of their own, the lit pages; only they keep their lighting layers, in the same rectangles as their diffuse blocks (`bright` is left out). The page is the unit the runtime streams: the pack is opened from its header, section table and `FRMS` (`kp_hd_pack_head_size`, `kp_hd_pack_open`), and a page's blocks are read at `DIFF` and `TEAM`'s offsets plus the page's index times its size, a lit page's layers at `NRML`, `AODP`, `SPEC` and `EMIS`'s (`kp_hd_page_offsets`). On 1.23.10.13515 HD2 has 50 pages, 20 lit, 540 MB of packs; HD 193 pages, 72 lit, 2.0 GB (schema 2, without the layers, packed the lit and unlit images together: HD2 48 pages, 354 MB with the tilesets; HD 189 pages, 1.4 GB, the frames filling 80 % and 82 % of them; schema 1 packed each image's sheet whole into 4096² and 8192² pages, 51–75 % full, 498 MB and 1.9 GB).

**`HDTS`** (`koprulu/<set>/tilesets/<name>.kpd`): `HEAD` (u16 tile count: the tileset's 1.17 megatiles, u16 tile size: 64 or 128, FourCC `DXT1`, or `ETC2` for ETC2 RGB8 blocks, also in a phone's ASTC build), `TILE` (each megatile's blocks; tile *i* is megatile *i* of `.vf4`/`.vx4`, Remastered's appended tiles left out); where the tileset has water (`TileSet/<name>.tmsk`: `KSMT`, u16 version, u16 count, then u16 megatile and u16 frame of `<name>_mask.dds.grp`), `WMAP` (u16 per megatile: 0 dry, else 1 + its mask) and `WMSK` (the masks, tile size squared bytes each: the BC1 frame's red, 255 water; empty and repeated ones left out). On 1.23.10.13515 `badlands`, `jungle`, `ashworld` (its lava), `desert`, `ice` and `twilight` have masks; `install` and `platform` none. `EDGE`: creep's edge frames (`TileSet/<name>.dds.grp`, 37 DXT1 images with one-bit alpha, the variant's scale times the SD frames' sizes or a little larger): u16 frame count, then each frame decoded into a tile of the tile size squared RGBA8 texels, transparent where it draws nothing, at the SD frame's offset in `tileset\<name>.grp` times the scale moved in so it stays in the tile; `kp_hd_etc2` keeps it as it is.

**`ui.kpd` (`HDUI`)**: the interface's HD art. Since ADR 0019's stage C6 the runtime lays it out from a Remastered install at start (`native/kp_app/kp_hd_ui_scr.c`: the frames' sizes and formats from their DDS headers, their places from the placement table `defs/scr/hd-ui-<profile>.json`, compiled as `SHUI`, which `kp_convert hd-ui-table` makes with the heuristics below; a file whose CASC content key is not the table's stays SD), and `kp_convert repack` writes the same container byte for byte into `koprulu-ui-<variant>.mpq`, its head (up to `FRMS`'s end) in the core ([the repack](repack.md)); the Python converter's `hd_ui.py` wrote it into `generated/hd/<variant>/` until then. The table's entries: per variant its HD files with their content keys, the frames that moved with their places, the consoles' centres. The GRPs and PCX of `ui.kpd` (its dialog GRPs, PCX and glue files) that have HD art, frame for frame. A GRP's `*.dds.grp` (`unit\cmdbtns\cmdicons.grp` from `unit/cmdicons/`, a race's `cmdbtns.grp` and `dlgs\<race>.grp` from `game/consoles/<race>/`), a PCX's `.DDS` when it is the SD picture drawn again (the correlation of their luminance gradients, the HD art centred on the SD picture, at least 0.5: Remastered drew most glue screens anew for its own layout, and those stay SD; the score screens' `untab.pcx` strips (SD 93 × 4) stay SD too, `hd_ui.SD_ONLY`: Remastered's strip is wider and laid over the SD panel it hid the tabs' notches), a console PCX (`game\<z|t|p>console.pcx`) five frames: its three pieces `console_left`, `_center`, `_right` and the two wings' tiles (`hd_ui.wing_tile`: a part of the piece's wing with no feature that catches the eye, its far end crossfaded into its start, PNG; the runtime repeats them), the pieces on the screen's bottom: the outer ones at the edges of an 853-wide screen around the 640 columns, the centre one where its luminance edges over the status panel's columns (138–408) agree best with the SD console's (a search of ±12 logical pixels from column 138). `HEAD` (u8 scale, u16 assets, u32 frames), `ASET` (per asset a 64-byte NUL-padded lowercase SD path, u32 first frame, u16 frames, u16 flags: 1 the console's pieces), `FRMS` (per frame u32 offset into `IMGS`, u32 size, u16 width, height in HD pixels, u8 format: 0 BC1, 1 BC3, 2 PNG RGBA, u8 0, i16 x, y: the image's top-left in HD pixels from the top-left of the SD frame it replaces, a GRP frame's, a PCX's or the console PCX's), `IMGS`; schema 3 (the other HD files 2). The runtime draws every frame at that offset (`kp_hd_ui_frame_place`); `kp_convert hd-ui-table` decides it (`place_frame`, `native/kp_convert/kp_hd_ui_table.c`): a GRP frame lies centred on its SD frame's trimmed rectangle (most are exactly twice its size in HD2), a wireframe (its square layers side by side) centred on the GRP canvas, unless the frame's opaque texels agree (intersection over union, the SD mask at the scale against the HD alpha at least half, a wireframe's layers ORed) by 0.3 better at another of those places or the canvas's origin, or 0.95 there: the minimap's `game\blink.grp` (frames 1–19 kept from the canvas's origin), the placeholder wireframes' text (`BLANK`, the unused units' names) and a few frames drawn again a pixel or two aside, 62 of HD2's 2,180 frames (the log names them); frames drawn anew agree at most 0.18 better elsewhere and keep their place. A picture lies centred on its SD one. On 1.23.10.13515 HD2 has 60 assets, 2,174 frames, 35 MB (128 pictures drawn anew and the 6 strips left out; HD 64 assets, 2,178 frames, 142 MB); the pieces sit at x −106, 135–144 and 172–183. Phones get it as it is (decoded on the CPU into the atlas).

**The HD portraits** (no container since ADR 0019's stage C6, which removed `portraits.kpd`, `HDPV`): each portrait movie of the portrait set (`portrait\<unit>\<name><variant>.smk`) is Remastered's WebM under its own name (`HD2/portrait/<unit>/<name><variant>.webm`, else the HD one; `kp_hd_ui_scr_clip_name`), read from the install or from the repack's `koprulu-portraits.mpq`, the SD movie's frame n the clip's frame n (its last past its end). Both variants take HD2's clips (224 × 240; 17 are 112 × 120): Remastered's HD console shows a portrait about 200 × 240 pixels wide at 4 ×, which they fill, and HD's 448 × 480 would be four times the bytes. The runtime reads a clip when its movie is first shown and decodes its frames on the CPU (`kp_webm.c`: nestegg and libvpx). On 1.23.10.13515: 621 of 621 movies, 72.8 MB. Remastered's HD glue movies are not taken: the main menu's are drawn for its own layout (`glue/mainmenu/single.webm` 1700 × 700 against the SD 320 × 116), the campaign's are the SD movies' size.

**`HDWA`** (`koprulu/<set>/water.kpd`): the water's normal maps, `effect/water_normal_1.dds.grp` (the large waves; HD2 45 frames of 64²) and `_2` (the detail; 120 frames of 256²): `HEAD` (u16 frames, u16 size of each set, FourCC `DXT1` or `ETC2`), `NRMA`, `NRMB` (their blocks, frame after frame).

**The web (stage R4b; ADR 0019, stages C4 to C6).** `tools/product.sh web` lays the repack's ranged groups beside the page and lists them with their sizes in `generated/web_sizes.txt` (`path size` per group, which `kp_app_web_beside` reads): the cinematics' (a movie fetched alone by its range in its group), the HD set's, `koprulu-ui-hd2.mpq` and `koprulu-portraits.mpq` ([the repack](repack.md)). The packs' and `ui.kpd`'s heads are in the core, which the page preloads; a page's layers, an interface frame and a portrait's clip are each fetched alone by their ranges, the tileset and the water whole (`kp_app_fetch.c`, `kp_app_hd.c`, `kp_app_hd_ui.c`). The server must answer Range requests (`tools/web_serve.py`); one that answers 200 costs the whole file per range.

**Phones (formats 1 and 2).** `kp_convert repack --hd hd2-astc,hd2-etc2` writes HD2's sets transcoded for phones (`native/kp_convert/kp_hd_transcode.c`, `kp_present/kp_etc.c`; ADR 0019, stage C5; `tools/product.sh` transcoded the converter's files with `kp_hd_phone` before it), each container kept in the cache by its BC bytes' SHA-256, the format and the codecs' settings (`KP_HD_TRANSCODE_SETTINGS`: a change of what a transcoding writes takes another). `tools/product.sh android|ios|ios-sim` carries one of them: `KOPRULU_PHONE_BLOCKS=astc|etc2` picks it, `astc` by default, `etc2` on `ios-sim`, whose context takes no ASTC. The frames and the other header fields stay, lit pages included. `kp_hd_phone` (`native/kp_core/tools/kp_hd_phone.c`) measures the blocks' choice through the same codecs (`--report`, `--report-tiles`) and checks them (`--selftest`).

| Section | BC (0) | ETC2 (1) | ASTC (2) | 2048² page |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `DIFF` | BC3 | ETC2 RGBA8 (EAC alpha) | 4 x 4 | 4 MB each |
| `TEAM` | BC1 | EAC R11 | 8 x 8 | 2 MB, 2 MB, 1.05 MB |
| `NRML` | BC3 | EAC RG11: x, y | EAC RG11 | 4 MB each |
| `AODP` | BC3 | EAC R11: the occlusion | EAC R11 | 4 MB, 2 MB, 2 MB |
| `SPEC`, `EMIS` | BC1 | ETC2 RGB8 | 6 x 6 | 2 MB, 2 MB, 1.87 MB |

The tiles (`HDTS`) and the water's normal maps (`HDWA`) are ETC2 RGB8 with either: a tile is a whole number of blocks only at ASTC's 4 x 4 and 8 x 8. kp_gfx swizzles EAC RG11's green into alpha and the occlusion's EAC R11 red into green, so the shader reads each format alike; WebGL has no swizzle, so a browser refuses RG11's blocks and draws such pages decoded and unlit. ETC2's colour modes are ETC1's individual and differential, the colour error weighted by alpha; ASTC comes from Arm's encoder (`-thorough`, the host's tool only). Every GLES 3.0 takes ETC2 and EAC (S3TC is an extension many phones lack); a device without ASTC draws SD from an ASTC build. HD2's packs: 442 MB in ASTC, 501 MB in ETC2, and the tilesets and water 79 MB. Against the BC pages, HD2 in dB: ETC2's colour 31–34, alpha 46–54; ASTC's 34–38 and 44–52, its alpha weighted up; the normal maps' x and y 51 and 47; the occlusion 50; the masks (0 and 255) exact ([ADR 0013](../adr/0013-remastered-data-source-and-hd.md), After R5, the phones' pages).

## Cinematics: no container since ADR 0019's stage C4

Until stage C4 `python3 -m converter cinematics` wrote `generated/video/cinematics-<sd|hd2>.kpd` (kind `CINE`, schema 2: `HEAD`, `CLIP`, `FRMS`, `PREL`, `SUBS`, `DATA`), each WebM's VP9 packets and Ogg Vorbis split out by FFmpeg, and transcoded the 1.17 install's Smacker movies with FFmpeg and oggenc into the same pack. Since then the player reads the WebM itself (`kp_webm_streams`, `kp_cine_movie_webm`: the VP9 packets and their frame table, the Vorbis packets as an Ogg stream that ends at the samples the blocks decode to less the last block's `DiscardPadding`), from a Remastered install or from the repack's cinematics groups ([the repack](repack.md): Remastered's WebM as they are, the 1.17 movies transcoded by `kp_convert`), and a 1.17 install's Smacker files as they are (ADR 0018, stage D7). The subtitles are the `.srt` files of the language in effect, else the install's text locale's, else enUS (`KP_LOCALE_SUBTITLES`, read when a movie opens). The movies' table and the briefings' preludes (`PlayCinematicWithPrelude` `0x458fc0`'s table `0x4edad8`) are `CINM`, compiled from `defs/campaign/cinematics.json` (`tools/defs_c.py`).

The player refuses a movie whose video is not VP9 or whose sound is not Vorbis, whose frame rate is none or has a term above `KP_CINE_MAX_RATE_TERM`, larger than `KP_CINE_MAX_SIDE`, whose first frame is not a key frame, with a packet of 0 bytes (libvpx refuses one), or whose packets' times stray more than half a frame from its constant rate (the real SD and HD2 movies stray at most 0.015 frame).

SD movies are VP9 profile 1 (4:4:4, `gbrp`, full range), coded 320 wide and shown 640 wide (a pixel of 2:1; 640 × 308 is the original's size); their frame counts equal the retail SMKs'. The HD2 movies are 960 × 540, 4:2:0, studio range; their `Pre*` movies run at 29.97 fps, `blizzard` is SD's 320 × 308 (shown 640 wide), and some frame counts differ from the SD set's (`starintr` 2081 against 2082), so a clip's rate and count are its own. On 1.23.10.13515: SD 28 movies, 190 MB; HD2 329 MB (the repack's groups: 190.2 and 329.4 MB, copies). The sound's length equals the video's (the intro: 138.80 s of Vorbis, 2082 frames at 15.0015 fps). libvpx's output equals FFmpeg's (`docs/adr/0015-video-decoder.md`).
