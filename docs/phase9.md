# Phase 9 progress

Plan: `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` §7 phase 9 (original-style UI and briefings). Started 2026-10-24, after the user approved the design (inline design document, 2026-10-24):
- the acceptance has three levels, so that the oracle does not hold a modern layout to the original's 640×480 screen:
  1. behaviour: the raw input of the emulator's games (mouse and keys by frame) goes through Koprulu's interface and gives the same commands, CRT draws, sound requests and portraits in the same frames; the interface takes its input in the coordinates of the original's dialogs, so this does not depend on the layout;
  2. panel content: every panel (command card, status and portrait, minimap, resources, messages, leaderboard, dialogs, the glue screens) is drawn into an index surface of its own, which equals the crop of the emulator's 640×480 frame at the panel's rectangle, compared in palette indices;
  3. composition: where the panels go, their scale, the fillers and the layouts are free, checked by geometric tests and the checklist; the `classic` 640×480 layout is a debugging configuration, not the acceptance;
- panels show the original's content by default; alternative panel variants (portrait screens, a large command card) and opt-in extensions (tooltips, a larger scale) are allowed as `approximation` (ADR 0011); the rules behind them are always the original's;
- the interface adapts to any aspect ratio and resolution from 9A on (scale from the window and DPI, independent anchored panels, layouts as data, a geometric test over 4:3 to 32:9, 9:16, 3:4 and resolutions up to 5120×1440, live resize and rotation);
- the screens are the campaign's path (main menu, single player, campaign, episode and mission selection, the mission history, the ready rooms with the briefing, victory and defeat, the score screen, the in-game menus); multiplayer, custom games and replays are out of V1;
- no cinematics in the default build: a campaign's cinematic rows are skipped (the plan's open question 2); SMK files play only in the briefing rooms (`glurdy*`) and as portraits. The cinematics play only in a build with `-DKP_CINEMATICS=ON` over Remastered's WebM packs (phase 10, 10H, [ADR 0015](adr/0015-video-decoder.md));
- the interface's rules that read the game or draw its generators (button conditions, selection under the cursor and in a box, the status panel, unit responses through the CRT, portraits, the briefing VM, the channel manager) live in `kp_core` as a `kp_ui` layer (ADR 0010); layout, drawing and playback are Lua and Defold; the interface's clock is passed in (the emulator's virtual ticks in the oracle).

## Notes

- The supply text is `(used + 1) / 2` over `(max + 1) / 2`, rounded up (`0x4a7640`), red (colour 6) when the maximum is below the used supply; the plan's "divided by two" is refined by the atlas (P36, P37).
- After a talking portrait opens, the previous-variant byte holds the opened variant minus 1 (`0xff` for variant 0), `0x4a6c50`.

- The console is not drawn into the screen bitmap `0x6d0b88`: its dialogs are blitted straight into Storm's surface 0 (`0x6cfbdc`), and the game view reaches the surface from the bitmap through the HUD mask. A screen capture therefore reads the surface `UnlockPrimarySurface 0x4cb6d0` last unlocked; the cursor stays drawn there (the compositor restores the background under it only in the bitmap).
- The `statfluf.bin` dialogs only repeat the console art (CaptureConsoleBackground over the console's parts the game view's dirty rectangles reach); Koprulu draws the console art whole under the panels, so they are not panels of their own, and the console can be cut into groups at columns no other panel crosses.
- A GUI component cannot show the panels: a GUI script runs in a Lua context of its own and a message carries about 2 KB. The canvas is one texture of the logical screen, written into the index target by the render script (ADR 0011 item 2); `kp_core.copy_to_buffer` (the Defold extension) fills the upload's buffer from a string.
- The logical screen holds at least 640 × 480 pixels (`presentation/ui/layout.lua`), the original's screen, so the console always fits; the integer scale is the largest whole factor that keeps that size, the fractional one fits the smaller side. Before phase 9 the integer mode rounded the window's height over 480 and could give a 1280 × 720 window a 640 × 360 view.
- The art of the interface (command icons, wireframes, cursors, dialog frames, consoles, glue backgrounds, room movies, music) is converted in the sub-stage that first draws or plays it, where its output is checked at once, rather than all in 9.0.
- The portrait's static draws one value of the portrait generator per pixel at every repaint of its rectangle, so the portrait is exact only when the invalid region is: a control under two of Storm's rectangles is drawn twice. Two causes of extra repaints were found this way: RefreshStatusUi's counter bumped on every visit of an order instead of in the original's branches (the status panel redrawn every frame, the region cut differently), and a local selection applied twice by the replay.
- Where the interface's work lies among the game's calls matters: a paused game's frames run the triggers inside the idle loop (a trigger portrait opens between two timer passes), and the Escape skip comes from a key event. The emulator's input log marks each such call of ui.jsonl at its place (`{"ui": ...}`: the paused frames, the release, the skip, the screen's draws), and the replay runs it there and hands the game's events to the interface at once.
- A fresh profile has the subtitles option off (`0x6d3148 & 0x400`): a trigger text shows only with its action's "always" flag (Display Text `0x4b6300`, Transmission `0x4b60e0`).
- The unit pool's creation errors (`0x425020`) and the unplaced unit's message (`0x46b070`, string `0x12`) are rez\network.tbl strings (`0x43f0c0` reads `0x665de0`), not stat_txt.
- `trigger_outcome` stalls in the emulator under screen captures after its Victory (the result dialog); its captures stop at frame 125.
- The game loop scrolls the map every 10 ms or more of its clock, not every frame (`0x4451f0`), so the scroll's records (`input.jsonl` "scroll") stand where UpdateMapScroll `0x49bf10` left its state changed; the replay runs the scroll there with the system cursor the record saw. The edge test reads `GetCursorPos`, not the last event's position: a scenario click above the view leaves the system cursor at y −20 and the event's at 479, and the original scrolls up (`patrol_cliff`); the cursor's choice records carry that position too.
- The view's limits are `(W − 20) × 32` and `(H − 12) × 32 + 8` (InitScreenScroll, P03), not the map less 640 × 400.
- The dialogs take an event before DispatchUiEvent reads the tick the log keeps, so the log's view (`scr`) is the one after the event, and a tooltip the event shows is logged before the event.
- While the tooltip shows, PresentFrame invalidates its rectangle at every frame (step 3 of `0x4c5360`): the dialogs under it are repainted each frame, which reaches the portrait's static when the box lies over the portrait.
- PlayUnitSelectSound `0x446c30` takes its branch from a completed building, but the option bit, the talking request and the annoyance count from units.dat's building flag alone: a building under construction answers like a unit without talking.
- A critter selected thirteen times in a row dies (offline): the interface's call to the game (`kp_critter_annoyed`), whose death sound plays inside the call, before the answer (`play_call_sounds`, `kp_event_peek`); test scenario `critter_annoyed`.

The engine still gets these keys, and the Command key's shortcuts stay the browser's, and so do AltGr's (Ctrl+Alt) and, on a Mac, Option's and Ctrl's combinations (no browser default there): a prevented keydown has no keypress, and the characters typed with them (`@`, `{`, `[` on a German keyboard) would never reach the text box or a save's name.
    - A focus change also forgets the modifiers' sides the viewer holds (`play_keys.reset`): a side released outside the window would keep the other side's release from the window, and its key byte down.
    - The cheats, the mission warp and the save dialog's name were typed live.
  - **Review fix.** `u:control` gives `bin_name` only to a control made from a BIN: a list's scroll bar made in code read `bins[-1]`, which the ASan suite caught.

- SaveGameFile's mutations under the emulator, two test scenarios saved through the menus with every step equal to the original's (`tools/state_check.py --kind scenarios`) and their raw input replayed by kp_ui (`tools/input_check.py`):
  - `save_effects`: Mutalisks against Marines, saved while a Glave Wurm's previous target is dying. SaveBullets `0x40a720` drops the reference: its decode `0x428b50` takes Die with state 1 as dead. In the original and in Koprulu it is 0 at the step after the save, while the same fight unsaved keeps it two steps more.
  - `save_ai_records`: `ai_town_new_units` saved after its worker record was freed. SaveAiSaveData `0x48ddb0` zeroes the free record's payload: its unit, town and type were still set and are 0, -1 and 0 after the save, in both.
  - A save is always the game paused under a modeless dialog. In single player it can never meet a placement, because OpenModelessDialog `0x46ecc0` ends the placement and the targeting first.
  - A bullet's source is never dead at a save: the game clears it when its unit dies (the Dragoons' runs).
  - The military decoder's drop of a record's region (UnpackMilitaryPool `0x4a9e40`) meets only free records, whose payload PackMilitaryPool `0x4a9f20` has just zeroed. The decoder restores the unit unchecked (`0x428ba0`, not the dead test of `0x428b50`), so a record of a dying unit keeps it, and an in-use record always has a unit: AddMilitaryAi `0x4ae760` sets it and nothing clears it. Koprulu's free records take the same values, and every field is set again when a record is taken.
  - Both runs are in the input oracle's runs (`$KOPRULU_INPUT`), so CI's `input_check` replays them.
