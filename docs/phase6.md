# Phase 6 progress

Plan: phase 6 (economy and production). Started 2026-10-17, after the user approved the design (inline design document, 2026-10-17):
- the state of harvesting, production and construction lives in the raw CUnit fields the native game already mirrors (the queue words and head, the current child `+0xec`, the type-dependent union read through `kp_union_get`/`kp_union_set`); the players gain the unit availability, the upgrade maxima and the research and upgrade in-progress bits, the PlayerAi records their available budget, the game the price cache of the cost checks (`0x53dad0`, `0x53db00`, `0x53db30`) and the whole-map rectangle `0x543228`, which a dropped powerup clamps in place; the save format stays version 1 and grows with them;
- the frame stays strictly sequential: the new orders join the existing dispatchers (`kp_order_dispatch.c`), the new commands the command executor (`kp_commands.c`);
- the AI's records of workers, buildings and towns belong to the AIScript VM of plan phase 7: a branch that reads one is ported but unreachable without it, and what needs the VM fails with its address (the computer's worker bonus `0x482f40`, a computer's new unit joining its AI `0x48d320`, the training tags of a computer's producer `0x484f70`/`0x484f90`); M0E's AI-driven E1/E2 move to phase 7 (plan correction);
- the oracle is the full-game emulator's scenarios played with the interface's inputs (selection boxes, hotkeys, right clicks, Escape) and the idle campaign runs; the acceptance of the phase is `terran01` played to victory by `sc_emulator scenarios.wasteland()` equal at every step.

**Review fixes (2026-10-18).**
- The emulator's log leaves out the game's own closes of the placement mode (those within StepObjects and the trigger scheduler, which Koprulu makes at the same point of its step) and holds the CRT draws of frame 0 until the game is past it, also over a frame-0 event before the game's seed; `protoss_build`, `terran_addon` and `terran_landing` recorded again log the same events.
- The paths that only failed loudly before and had no scenario now have one: the Cancel Nuke command and a missile dying with its Silo (`nuke_cancel`), a computer Ghost's strike (`ai_ghost_nuke`), BuildNydusExit (`nydus_exit`).
- The requirement evaluator's program override (a test hook) drops the offsets resolved so far whenever it is set or cleared.
- Shared helpers: the busy-or-researching test of the token 0xff07 and the lift-off (`kp_unit_occupied`), the landing error voice (`kp_cannot_land_voice`), the nuke types of the removal (`kp_nuke_linked_type`).

**Design corrections (2026-10-17, continued).**
- The local player's building placement mode is interface state the game consults, as the local selection is: the Psi fields it shows for a Protoss builder are sprites the state compares, and the game closes the mode when the builder can no longer place (`kp_build_mode.c`). The emulator logs its starts and the interface's closes (`ui.jsonl` "placement", `StartPlacement 0x47d3a0`, `ClosePlacement 0x47d580`); the game's own closes (CanPlaceCurrent after an order or a removal, an emptied selection, a result dialog) come within StepObjects and the trigger scheduler that follows it, which Koprulu runs itself, so the emulator leaves them out of the log rather than have Koprulu replay them at the head of the frame; a replayed close cancels an active mode only, as every caller of the original does. Its cursor, prompt and ghost images (gas buildings, add-ons) are the presentation's: the state decoder leaves the ghost and box pools' images out of the sprites.
- The creep tile art shares the CRT `rand` with the interface (the unit voices' choice `0x447130`/`0x447190`): the emulator logs every draw outside the creep callback after the game's seed ("crt") and Koprulu draws alike at the same frame; the draws of frame 0 are held until the first event or object step past it, so that the game's seed drops those of the menus even when a frame-0 event (the briefing's objectives) comes before the seed. The interface's unit voices draw from the game's CRT generator the same way (`kp_ui_voice.c`, phase 9).
- A flying building that dies while it lands lets go of the site it reserved (`0x41a190` at the order point, `0x42510d`); the remove path failed there before `kp_lift.c` made the case reachable (`terran_landing`). Two behaviours that look like defects are the original's and stay: CreatePsiFieldSprite `0x42f270` shows all fields through `0x430320`, which sets `0x665b64` only after its loop, so a hide that meets a selected provider without a field shows them again; CreateUnitWithPlacementCheck `0x4261f0` stamps a building's creep before it places the unit, so one that does not fit leaves its creep.
- Order 155 is the Capture the Flag beacon's (`0x47da50`), not a nuke's (the constant was misnamed).
- The state decoder reads a sprite pointer in the union only in a Pylon's `+0xd0` (an ordinary value can equal one), and a Ghost's `+0xd0` as its nuke dot's lone thingy (`t<index>`).
