# Implementation status

The traceability ledger of `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` §6 and §8. Each implemented contract is linked to its research domain (R01–R37 of the [completion ledger](research/completion-ledger.md)), its evidence, the Koprulu code and tests, and the next gate it has not passed. State as of phase 6 (2026-10-17): economy and production done, items 6.0–6.6.

The marks are independent axes, not one scale (§8):
- `original-static`: data or instructions decoded;
- `original-isolated`: the original function or chain ran to its return on declared inputs;
- `implemented` / `fixture-verified`: Koprulu code exists and passes the named tests;
- `original-game-observed`: compared with a full-game observation of the original;
- `runtime-verified`: checked in the running product (Defold until phase S, SDL3 since);
- `approximation`: a deliberate difference with its ADR.

`terran/terran01` played to victory by the emulator's scripted play equals the original at every step ([phase 6](phase6.md)); the initial state of every mission equals the original, its trigger state included, and twelve campaign missions without AI scripts (`terran/tutorial`, `terran/terran01`, `terran/terran02`, `terran/terran04`, `zerg/zerg01`, `zerg/zerg05` of [phase 5](phase5.md); `terran/terran09`, `zerg/zerg07`, `protoss/protoss04`, `protoss/protoss06`, `expprotoss/protoss02`, `expzerg/zerg01` of [phase 6](phase6.md)) run idle for 2,000 steps equal to the original at every 20th step, their own triggers and trigger trace included; with the AIScript VM and the AI runtime of [phase 7](phase7.md) all 64 campaign missions run idle for 2,000 steps equal to the original at every 20th step, their trigger and AI traces included, and a run saved at step 1001 in the middle of AI work continues as the original.
