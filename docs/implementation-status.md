# Implementation status

The traceability ledger of `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` §6 and §8. Each implemented contract is linked to its research domain (R01–R37 of the [completion ledger](research/completion-ledger.md)), its evidence, the Koprulu code and tests, and the next gate it has not passed. State as of phase 3, items 3.2–3.4 in progress (2026-10-12).

The marks are independent axes, not one scale (§8):
- `original-static`: data or instructions decoded;
- `original-isolated`: the original function or chain ran to its return on declared inputs;
- `implemented` / `fixture-verified`: Koprulu code exists and passes the named tests;
- `original-game-observed`: compared with a full-game observation of the original;
- `runtime-verified`: checked in running Defold;
- `approximation`: a deliberate difference with its ADR.

No mission has a gameplay status yet: the initial state of every mission equals the original, and the frame loop runs idle units, the select, Stop, Hold Position and right-click move commands, path search and ground movement of single units and groups, including collisions between units and with terrain and the flight of air units with their repulsion, like the original (nine scenarios, every step), but the campaign missions need the AI (phase 5), subunits and further orders from their second step on.
