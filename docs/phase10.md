# Phase 10 progress

Plan: `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` §7 phase 10, items 1 and 2 (touch UX; safe areas, DPI, rotation and split-screen on the fly). Started 2026-10-28, after the user approved the plan ([ADR 0014](adr/0014-touch-input-and-zoom.md)):
- the touch layer translates gestures into the inputs the interface already takes and changes no rule (the oracles stay as they are);
- a tap is a smart tap (select one's own unit, else order when one's own units are selected, else select); a long press does the other one;
- buttons fire on the lift inside them, as in the original;
- the world zooms in only, from 1× to 2×, as presentation;
- the cursor is hidden in the touch mode except while a target or a placement is aimed; no edge scroll.

The optimisation items 3–6 of phase 10 are not part of this work.

## 10A: the touch layer

- **Fingers, not a synthetic mouse.** `SDL_HINT_TOUCH_MOUSE_EVENTS` is off; `SDL_EVENT_FINGER_*` of a direct touch device (a screen: a trackpad's indirect touches stay the mouse's) go to `kp_touch` at the logical screen's point, kept below a pixel; mouse events with `SDL_TOUCH_MOUSEID` are dropped. The last real device sets the input mode (`a->touch_mode`).
- **The gestures** (`kp_touch.c`, no SDL): a panel's touch moves the interface's pointer there and presses, a move drags, the lift releases (the original's buttons fire on a lift inside them; a quick second press nearby is a double click, as `CS_DBLCLKS`); a game-view touch waits for its lift: within the slop (10 dp) a tap, held 450 ms a long press, past the slop the box (a left press at the touch, the moves, the release). The tap is the smart tap: `kp_ui_touch_target` (read-only) answers select (a unit of the local player's under the point, or none of the player's selected), order (the player's units selected) or aim (a target or a placement being chosen); a select tap is a left click (the second within 400 ms and 24 dp a double click: the kind on the screen), an order tap a right click, a long press the other one. Aiming at a target, the cursor shows under the finger and follows it, the lift clicks there; a placement's outline follows the finger and stays where it is lifted, and a tap on it places the building (a click at the outline's point; `kp_ui_placement_at`: the footprint as the preview shows it), a tap elsewhere moves it, a drag on it moves it along with the finger's offset (`test_touch.placement_waits_for_a_tap_on_it`, `test_hud.touch_placement_knows_its_ghost`). A second finger ends a game-view gesture without its click (the box through the capture's loss) and pans the view with the fingers' middle; over a panel's press it does nothing.
- **No cursor, no edge scroll.** A touch frame updates no cursor (`kp_hud_frame`'s `KP_HUD_POINTER_NONE`), so the edge scroll never starts and the cursor is not drawn; aiming updates the pointer's cursor alone (`kp_ui_pointer_update`: the targeting's crosshair, no edge arrow, with which the game view would take no click). Entering the touch mode sets the cursor id back to the arrow.
- **Android's back** (`SDL_HINT_ANDROID_TRAP_BACK_BUTTON`, `SDL_SCANCODE_AC_BACK`) is the glue's Escape (its back); in a game `kp_hud_back_key` makes it Escape where that closes or cancels something (a menu over the game, a target's or a placement's aiming, a message's text box) and else F10, the game menu: the game's own Escape skips the transmissions (SkipLocalTriggerWaits), which the first Fold7 play took for a Back that did nothing (`test_hud.back_opens_and_closes_the_game_menu`).
- **A tap on a minimap ping** (within `KP_HUD_PING_DP` = 24 dp of its centre) centres the view on its event: the Space's jump to the last event for the fingers (ours, the user's choice: a touch away from the pings stays the minimap's). The ping keeps its event's map point (`KpUiPing.map_x/y`, `kp_ui_minimap_ping_near`); `kp_hud_touch_target` answers a host button there (`test_hud.touch_ping_centres_its_event`).
- **A trigger's camera pan steps with no pointer.** `kp_hud_frame` stepped the scroll (the keys' scroll, a trigger's pan toward its view) only in the mouse's frames, so in the touch mode a trigger's Center View never arrived: the pan held the view (the minimap and the portrait's centring did nothing after the first one), and its trigger waited forever, with the player's later transmissions and waits (Boot Camp's Refinery trigger kept switch 6 set, so its victory never came). The frames with no pointer now step it with no edge scroll. A pan also arrives from a view off the original's 8-pixel grid (a two-finger pan's, a rotation's kept centre): its smallest step is 8 pixels and is limited to the distance left, so a view a few pixels short of the target stopped there and the pan held the view for good (the Fold7's second play: after Boot Camp's Barracks the view stayed on it, the minimap and the groups did nothing); the pan puts the view onto the grid first (`kp_ui_update_scroll`) (`test_hud.a_pan_ends_without_a_pointer`, from a view on and off the grid; `test_hud.touch_minimap_moves_the_view`, `touch_portrait_centres_the_view`).
- **Cancelled gestures:** the focus lost, or the screen the gesture began on changed (the glue and the game, the logical size: a rotation, a fold), lets every finger go without its click (`kp_touch_cancel`).
- **Tests:** `test_touch` (13: a panel's press and double press, the smart tap, the long press, the double tap, the box and its slop, the aim, the placement's confirmation, two fingers' cancel, pan and zoom, a panel's press under a second finger, the cancel, the density's thresholds, the host's buttons); `test_hud.touch_taps_select_and_order` (terran01: a tap selects a Marine, a tap on the ground orders a right click, a long press on another Marine orders onto it, the console is a panel's).

## 10B: the touch pad

- **What it is.** A translucent column of buttons along the game view's left edge under the resource line (`statres.bin`'s place), in the touch mode's games only: Shift, the groups' toggle ("1-0"), then the control groups with units in them (folded) or all ten, 1–9 and 0 (opened by the toggle). The first Fold7 play (2026-10-28) found the first form, a row of eleven over the game view's top, too large: it hid the resource line and showed groups seldom used; the user chose the folded column showing the groups held, Shift above the toggle so that the opening leaves it in place. Ours, not the original's (ADR 0014 point 4): `kp_ui_touch_pad` draws them with the tooltip box's outline (UI colour 16, ShowTooltipText 0x418f60), the translucent in-game dialogs' fill (the blend colour of BlitSingleColourBlend 0x4ca9b0, blended over the world as the game menu's background by `kp_ui_canvas_translucent`; the tooltip's opaque fill in a game started without the glue, whose theme sets the blend colour) and the game's font16, a group with units in it white, an empty one grey, the pressed button, an active Shift and the opened toggle yellow with a double outline, a locked Shift with a third. The canvas lays the pad over the panels as a layer of its own (`KpCanvas.overlay`, under the tooltip); `kp_hud` lays it out (`KpHudPad`: a button of `KP_HUD_PAD_DP` = 44 dp, its height four fifths of its width, shortened where the view's height is short of the column) and lays it out and draws it again only when its state or the groups held change.
- **Its buttons** are the touch layer's host buttons (`KP_TOUCH_BUTTON`: pressed at the touch, a tap at the lift, a long press when held): a group's tap is the digit's character (the group recalled; the original's second recall within 500 ms centres the view on it), its long press Ctrl and the digit with no Shift (the selection assigned, `ACC_ASSIGN_FIRST`; with the pad's Shift on the original's Ctrl, Shift and the digit assign nothing, so the long press leaves Shift out and Shift stays on; `test_hud.touch_pad_reassigns_a_held_group`); a tap folds the opened pad KP_HUD_PAD_FOLD_MS (600 ms) later, so that a second tap within the original's 500 ms still finds the button where it was and centres the view on the group (folding at once moved the button away; `test_hud.touch_pad_second_tap_centres_the_group`), a long press folds it at once; the folded pad shows a group from its assignment on; the toggle's tap opens or folds it; a lift off the button pressed does nothing. Shift's tap turns it on for the next click (a second tap turns it off): a click with it on that queues a command (an order, a selection's change, a group added to the selection) turns it off, a pad's button or a card's button that queues nothing (one that opens a targeting) leaves it on for the click that follows; a long press on it locks it on (`KP_HUD_SHIFT_LOCKED`, drawn with a third outline) for every click until a tap turns it off, the waypoints of a queue (the user's request). It first stayed on until tapped again, for the waypoints of a queue, but left on it went unnoticed and made the later taps and boxes add to the selection (the Fold7, 2026-10-28; the user chose the one click, as the plan first said; `test_hud.touch_pad_shift_lasts_one_click`). The interface's Shift byte is set while it is on, so that the clicks are the original's Shift clicks: an order queued, a tap or a box adding to the selection (a building is not added to units), and a Terran building's SCV takes no right click with it or without it (CanUnitTakeRightClick 0x47f4b0).
- **Tests:** `test_hud.touch_pad_assigns_and_recalls_groups`, `touch_pad_reassigns_a_held_group`, `touch_pad_shift_lasts_one_click` (the column under the resource line along the left edge with its buttons of 44 dp, its fill blended and its outline opaque, folded with no group shown, opened with all ten, its hits and gaps, a long press assigning group 1 and folding it, group 1 shown folded, a lift off the pressed button, Shift on and off, the pad gone off the touch mode), `test_touch.host_button_taps_and_holds`.

## 10E: safe areas, rotation, platforms

- **The view keeps its centre** when a game's logical screen changes size (a rotation, the Fold7's fold, split screen, a window resized; plan section 2.1): `kp_hud_keep_focus` moves the top-left by half the view's change, before the clamp; the camera was only clamped before, so a rotation swung the view about its top-left. Test: `test_hud.a_new_screen_keeps_the_view_centre`.
- **Android's safe area** (`tools/native/android_app.sh`, template edition 3) now leaves out the mandatory gesture zone too (the home bar's, which takes every touch) besides the system bars and the cutout: the panels and buttons never lie under it. The back gesture's side zones stay in it (they take swipes only; a tap there reaches the game).
- **Immersive mode on the phones:** the window is fullscreen (`SDL_SetWindowFullscreen`: on Android SDL's sticky immersive mode with the cutout mode `ALWAYS`; on iOS no status bar and the dimmed home indicator, a first swipe showing it, a second going home); the safe area keeps the picture off the cutouts.
- **iOS's Info.plist** (`native/kp_app/ios/Info.plist.in`, `MACOSX_BUNDLE_INFO_PLIST`): `UILaunchScreen` (without a launch screen iOS runs the app in an old screen size's compatibility mode), the portrait and both landscape orientations, no status bar.
- **Left as it is:** the world is not drawn under the cutouts and the rounded corners: the safe area's outside stays black, as before (drawing it would widen the world's view past the interface's, with the culling, the fog and the screen rectangle in the game's state to follow; TODO). The integer scale's logical screen still rounds up, so its picture may pass the safe area's top and right by up to scale − 1 pixels.
