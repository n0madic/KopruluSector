# Phase 10 progress

Plan: `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` §7 phase 10, items 1 and 2 (touch UX; safe areas, DPI, rotation and split-screen on the fly). Started 2026-10-28, after the user approved the plan ([ADR 0014](adr/0014-touch-input-and-zoom.md)):
- the touch layer translates gestures into the inputs the interface already takes and changes no rule (the oracles stay as they are);
- a tap is a smart tap (select one's own unit, else order when one's own units are selected, else select); a long press does the other one;
- buttons fire on the lift inside them, as in the original;
- the world zooms in only, from 1× to 2×, as presentation;
- the cursor is hidden in the touch mode except while a target or a placement is aimed; no edge scroll.

The optimisation items 3–6 of phase 10 are not part of this work; items 4 and 5 were audited on 2026-10-28 ([10G](#10g-pools-allocations-and-the-load-graph-audit)).

## 10A: the touch layer

- **Fingers, not a synthetic mouse.** `SDL_HINT_TOUCH_MOUSE_EVENTS` is off; `SDL_EVENT_FINGER_*` of a direct touch device (a screen: a trackpad's indirect touches stay the mouse's) go to `kp_touch` at the logical screen's point, kept below a pixel; mouse events with `SDL_TOUCH_MOUSEID` are dropped. The last real device sets the input mode (`a->touch_mode`).
- **The gestures** (`kp_touch.c`, no SDL): a panel's touch moves the interface's pointer there and presses, a move drags, the lift releases (the original's buttons fire on a lift inside them; a quick second press nearby is a double click, as `CS_DBLCLKS`); a game-view touch waits for its lift: within the slop (10 dp) a tap, held 450 ms a long press, past the slop the box (a left press at the touch, the moves, the release). The tap is the smart tap: `kp_ui_touch_target` (read-only) answers select (a unit of the local player's under the point, or none of the player's selected), order (the player's units selected) or aim (a target or a placement being chosen); a select tap is a left click (the second within 400 ms and 24 dp a double click: the kind on the screen), an order tap a right click, a long press the other one. Aiming at a target, the cursor shows under the finger and follows it, the lift clicks there; a placement's outline follows the finger and stays where it is lifted, and a tap on it places the building (a click at the outline's point; `kp_ui_placement_at`: the footprint as the preview shows it), a tap elsewhere moves it, a drag on it moves it along with the finger's offset (`test_touch.placement_waits_for_a_tap_on_it`, `test_hud.touch_placement_knows_its_ghost`). A second finger ends a game-view gesture without its click (the box through the capture's loss) and pans the view with the fingers' middle; over a panel's press it does nothing.
- **No cursor, no edge scroll.** A touch frame updates no cursor (`kp_hud_frame`'s `KP_HUD_POINTER_NONE`), so the edge scroll never starts and the cursor is not drawn; aiming updates the pointer's cursor alone (`kp_ui_pointer_update`: the targeting's crosshair, no edge arrow, with which the game view would take no click). Entering the touch mode sets the cursor id back to the arrow.
- **Android's back** (`SDL_HINT_ANDROID_TRAP_BACK_BUTTON`, `SDL_SCANCODE_AC_BACK`) is the glue's Escape (its back); in a game `kp_hud_back_key` makes it Escape where that closes or cancels something (a menu over the game, a target's or a placement's aiming, a message's text box) and else F10, the game menu: the game's own Escape skips the transmissions (SkipLocalTriggerWaits), which the first Fold7 play took for a Back that did nothing (`test_hud.back_opens_and_closes_the_game_menu`).
- **A tap on a minimap ping** (within `KP_HUD_PING_DP` = 24 dp of its centre) centres the view on its event: the Space's jump to the last event for the fingers (ours, the user's choice: a touch away from the pings stays the minimap's). The ping keeps its event's map point (`KpUiPing.map_x/y`, `kp_ui_minimap_ping_near`); `kp_hud_touch_target` answers a panel's host button there (`KP_TOUCH_PANEL_BUTTON`: a slide past the slop becomes the minimap's press and drag from the touch, so the ping takes no drag of the minimap's and a drag ends in no jump to the event; `test_hud.touch_ping_centres_its_event`, `test_touch.panel_button_slides_into_a_drag`).
- **A parked placement preview** stays through a two-finger look around (the second finger puts it back where it was parked: the first finger's touch had moved it to the finger) and through a pad button's tap (a host button moves no pointer); it goes when the placement ends (`KpTouchHost.placing`, `kp_hud_placing`), not when the point under it stops being the placement's (the pad or a ping over it; `test_touch.parked_preview_stays_through_a_pan`).
- **A trigger's camera pan steps with no pointer.** `kp_hud_frame` stepped the scroll (the keys' scroll, a trigger's pan toward its view) only in the mouse's frames, so in the touch mode a trigger's Center View never arrived: the pan held the view (the minimap and the portrait's centring did nothing after the first one), and its trigger waited forever, with the player's later transmissions and waits (Boot Camp's Refinery trigger kept switch 6 set, so its victory never came). The frames with no pointer now step it with no edge scroll. A pan also arrives from a view off the original's 8-pixel grid (a two-finger pan's, a rotation's kept centre): its smallest step is 8 pixels and is limited to the distance left, so a view a few pixels short of the target stopped there and the pan held the view for good (the Fold7's second play: after Boot Camp's Barracks the view stayed on it, the minimap and the groups did nothing); an axis of the pan less than a step from its target moves the rest of the way (`pan_remainder` in `kp_ui_scroll.c`, since the second review below; first the pan put the view onto the grid, which moved it up to 7 pixels away from the target before the step) (`test_hud.a_pan_ends_without_a_pointer`, from a view on and off the grid; `test_hud.touch_minimap_moves_the_view`, `touch_portrait_centres_the_view`).
- **Cancelled gestures:** the focus lost, or the screen the gesture began on changed (the glue and the game, the logical size: a rotation, a fold), lets every finger go without its click (`kp_touch_cancel`).
- **Tests:** `test_touch` (15: a panel's press and double press, the smart tap, the long press, the double tap, the box and its slop, the aim, the placement's confirmation, two fingers' cancel, pan and zoom, a panel's press under a second finger, the cancel, the density's thresholds, the host's buttons, a parked preview through a pan, a panel's button's slide); `test_hud.touch_taps_select_and_order` (terran01: a tap selects a Marine, a tap on the ground orders a right click, a long press on another Marine orders onto it, the console is a panel's).

## 10B: the touch pad

- **What it is.** A translucent column of buttons along the game view's left edge under the resource line (`statres.bin`'s place), in the touch mode's games only: Shift, the groups' toggle ("1-0"), then the control groups with units in them (folded) or all ten, 1–9 and 0 (opened by the toggle). The first Fold7 play (2026-10-28) found the first form, a row of eleven over the game view's top, too large: it hid the resource line and showed groups seldom used; the user chose the folded column showing the groups held, Shift above the toggle so that the opening leaves it in place. Ours, not the original's (ADR 0014 point 4): `kp_ui_touch_pad` draws them with the tooltip box's outline (UI colour 16, ShowTooltipText 0x418f60), the translucent in-game dialogs' fill (the blend colour of BlitSingleColourBlend 0x4ca9b0, blended over the world as the game menu's background by `kp_ui_canvas_translucent`; the tooltip's opaque fill in a game started without the glue, whose theme sets the blend colour) and the game's font16, a group with units in it white, an empty one grey, the pressed button, an active Shift and the opened toggle yellow with a double outline, a locked Shift with a third. The canvas lays the pad over the panels as a layer of its own (`KpCanvas.overlay`, under the tooltip); `kp_hud` lays it out (`KpHudPad`: a button of `KP_HUD_PAD_DP` = 44 dp, its height four fifths of its width, shortened where the view's height is short of the column) and lays it out and draws it again only when its state, the groups held or the blend colour change (the game menu's, the options', the saves' and the result's dialogs set their border's colour as the blend's, SetDialogBorderColor 0x47bd40: a fill drawn with the colour before was no longer the blend's and went opaque, the pad's buttons flickering under the Fold7's Victory; the pad is now hidden under these dialogs and drawn with the colour they leave once they close; `test_hud.touch_pad_follows_the_blend_colour`).
- **Its buttons** are the touch layer's host buttons (`KP_TOUCH_BUTTON`: pressed at the touch, a tap at the lift, a long press when held): a group's tap is the digit's character (the group recalled; the original's second recall within 500 ms centres the view on it), its long press Ctrl and the digit with no Shift (the selection assigned, `ACC_ASSIGN_FIRST`; with the pad's Shift on the original's Ctrl, Shift and the digit assign nothing, so the long press leaves Shift out and Shift stays on; `test_hud.touch_pad_reassigns_a_held_group`); a tap folds the opened pad KP_HUD_PAD_FOLD_MS (600 ms) later, so that a second tap within the original's 500 ms still finds the button where it was and centres the view on the group (folding at once moved the button away; `test_hud.touch_pad_second_tap_centres_the_group`), a long press folds it at once; the folded pad shows a group from its assignment on, until none of its units is left (`kp_ui_group_held`); the toggle's tap opens or folds it; a lift off the button pressed does nothing. Shift's tap turns it on for the next click (a second tap turns it off): a click with it on that queues a command (an order, a selection's change, a group added to the selection) turns it off, a pad's button or a card's button that queues nothing (one that opens a targeting) leaves it on for the click that follows; a long press on it locks it on (`KP_UI_PAD_SHIFT_LOCKED`, drawn with a third outline) for every click until a tap turns it off, the waypoints of a queue (the user's request). It first stayed on until tapped again, for the waypoints of a queue, but left on it went unnoticed and made the later taps and boxes add to the selection (the Fold7, 2026-10-28; the user chose the one click, as the plan first said; `test_hud.touch_pad_shift_lasts_one_click`). The interface's Shift byte is set while it is on, so that the clicks are the original's Shift clicks: an order queued, a tap or a box adding to the selection (a building is not added to units), and a Terran building's SCV takes no right click with it or without it (CanUnitTakeRightClick 0x47f4b0).
- **Out of the way.** The pad is hidden under a modeless dialog (the game menu, the options, the saves, the result: over them it hid their buttons and took their taps, `pad_shown`); an opened pad's fold waits while a button is pressed (laid out again it moved the button from under the finger and the tap was lost); a group's button does nothing while a text box has the focus (its digit was typed into the message); the window's focus lost or back clears the key bytes (`kp_ui_set_active`) and the pad's Shift with them (`kp_hud_keys_cleared`; it stayed drawn on while the clicks had no Shift); a view too short for the column at `KP_HUD_PAD_MIN_PX` (12 logical pixels) leaves the last groups out instead of reaching the console (`test_hud.touch_pad_stays_out_of_the_way`).
- **Tests:** `test_hud.touch_pad_assigns_and_recalls_groups`, `touch_pad_reassigns_a_held_group`, `touch_pad_shift_lasts_one_click` (the column under the resource line along the left edge with its buttons of 44 dp, its fill blended and its outline opaque, folded with no group shown, opened with all ten, its hits and gaps, a long press assigning group 1 and folding it, group 1 shown folded, a lift off the pressed button, Shift on and off, the pad gone off the touch mode), `test_touch.host_button_taps_and_holds`.

## 10E: safe areas, rotation, platforms

The ghost frames themselves depend on malloc handing the pointer back, so they did not show in the check's own screenshots before the fix.

## 10G: pools, allocations and the load graph (audit)

Plan phase 10 items 4 and 5, measured on macOS (the development build) with a dyld interposer counting `malloc`, `calloc` and `realloc` by the innermost frame in the executable, and the loader's own logs (neither is in the repository).

- **Simulation tick:** no allocation in `kp_core`'s step over 60,000–200,000 steps of zerg05, zerg09, terran09 and protoss07 (`kp_oracle play --mission`, the AI running); only the tool's own dump buffers allocate.
- **The playing app:** the allocations of the game's own code in steady state were `kp_ui_region` (a fragment vector grown and freed on every call: about 1 a second idle, 12 in combat; its fragments are now kept in the struct, `FRAG_INLINE` = 512, the heap only past them: `test_kp_ui.region_cuts_overlapping_rects`, `region_grows_past_its_inline_fragments`), `kp_vtext_quads` (growth, left) and `kp_smk_open` (a portrait's video, per event: nine blocks, the four header trees, their scratch and the frame; now one, the trees' entries and the pixels together, the scratch on the stack: `test_ui_data.smk_header_opens_in_one_block`; a header tree declaring more than 16 MB is refused so the sizes cannot overflow). The rest is the GL driver (macOS) and SDL's audio thread.
- **Image data upload:** values were already written only when they changed, but the texture's dirty rows were one run from the lowest to the highest, which spanned the families' separate slot ranges (about 42 rows uploaded for 5–8 changed in combat, of 73). Each family now has its own run (`KpImageData.dirty_lo/hi[]`), the runs that touch are joined, and `kp_image_data_take` returns them as `KpRowRange`s (`kp_gfx_image_rows` uploads each): `test_present.images_upload_the_changed_rows`, `images_join_the_runs_that_touch`.
- **Load graph (§2.2):** on the autotest's 64 missions (the catalog with the expansions) the packs in memory equal the `.kpm`'s `PACK` list, no sound pack is held outside the world, and only the current mission's speech is loaded; the heap after a world's release does not grow with the missions (a plateau of about 125 MB). The decoded `sfx` cache used to outlive the world (10 sounds by the autotest's end): `kp_audio_mission` now releases it with the trigger WAVs. The world loader no longer skips a pack the mission names but the data lacks, nor stops at `KP_APP_PACKS`: either fails the load with the file or the count in the message (it left the pack's sprites undrawn without a word; the 64 missions load as before, and a data directory without `zerg.kpd` fails the autotest's first mission).
- **Music:** `music.kpd` (31.5 MB) was read whole at start and kept for the whole run, outside the graph. The native product now reads only its track index at start (`native/kp_app/kp_music.c`: the file's head and the 25 tracks' small headers) and a track's bytes when it plays, one at a time (`kp_app_audio.c`; since the review fixes below a stream of the track's range in the file, decoded as it plays); the web keeps the container whole, as its files are in memory already. The macOS app's resident size in terran01 fell from 293 MB to 265 MB. `test_music.index_follows_the_tracks`, `index_refuses_a_track_past_its_section`, `index_matches_the_converted_music` (headers only are read: at most 5 bytes at a time). The web build compiles; the music itself was heard (a track loaded and played) on macOS only.
- **The heap's rise** (the resident size 90 to 125 MB over the autotest's first missions, an earlier reading taken at each mission's start; read again with the heap statistics the process is at 125 MB before the first world and 296–363 MB across the 64 missions, creeping up slowly) is not a leak: `leaks` at the end of three passes over the same 12 missions finds 398, 403 and 403 leaked blocks, 25 KB, all `NSXPCConnection` cycles of macOS's own App Intents, none the game's; malloc's live bytes (`malloc_zone_statistics`) when the same mission starts are the same in passes 2 and 3 (within 1 MB; pass 1 to 2 adds 2–3 MB, the first visits' caches) and across the 64 missions they follow the world's size (119–139 MB) with no trend; the GL texture names stay at 14. What grows is what malloc and the driver keep after a free: `Malloc Small (empty)` 2.5, 10.5 and 21 MB at the passes' ends (`vmmap -summary`), the driver's `IOAccelerator` 102.3, 102.4 and 104.6 MB, so the resident size gains a few MB per pass over the same missions. Not measured: whether that levels off over a long session on a phone.
- **Left:** the packs are per race, so 34 of 64 missions hold all six (22 MB on disk) and 29 five; `kp_vtext_quads` grows its buffer per call. Not measured: Android, the web, the HD art, the time of a track's read on a phone.
