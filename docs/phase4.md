# Phase 4 progress

> **Historical record.** Written before phase S ([phaseS.md](phaseS.md)), when the runtime was Defold with Lua. The Defold build server, `tools/native/build.sh` and `tools/spike.sh` named below were removed in phase S; the build is CMake (`CMakeLists.txt`, `tools/product.sh`).

Plan: phase 4 (combat and abilities). Started 2026-10-14, after the user approved the design (inline design document, 2026-10-14):
- the state of combat lives in the native game `KpGame` (fixed arrays in the original's record numbering, no allocation in a step); the save format stays version 1 and grows with it;
- the step stays strictly sequential in the original's order (unit timers, orders, images that fire, the bullet step, thingies);
- every sub-stage is checked against its oracles before the next one: per-step emulator scenarios (level C) and original-code probes (level A) for the branches a scenario cannot reach;
- unported branches keep failing with their addresses; M1 (`terran/terran01`) needs come first.

**Design correction (2026-10-14).** The design expected enemies "of a computer player without an AI script, which keeps only guard records". A computer player always brings the regional AI: its AI region records, the regional scheduler, the reaction of its units to attacks and their AI orders (Guard, AIGuard, ComputerAI, ComputerReturn). A second human slot is disabled in single player and a neutral slot is allied with everyone, so neutral targets (player 11) never fight back. The scriptless computer's AI is therefore part of item 4.2; the AI scripts, towns, workers, military groups and production stay in plan phase 7.

## Oracle

**Bullets in the state model.** The original's 100 Bullet records (`0x53db90`, `0x70` bytes each) appear in two categories of their own, so the goldens of the earlier categories stay valid: `bullets` (the allocated count `0x53db8c`, the active and free lists with their tails, `0x540750`/`0x540758`) and `bullet` (each active record, in list order). Koprulu keeps a bullet's flingy part in the unit field arrays at record `KP_UNITS + b` (owner, state at `+0x4d`, signal, order point and target included), so the flingy code moves units and bullets alike, and the rest of the record in `KpBullets`.

**The computer AI in the state model.** The emulator dumps each player's AI region array (`0x6b1a74[p]`, the blobs `ai_regions<p>`); the categories are `ai_region` (the records that differ from their start), `ai_reach` (`0x6b1aa0` with its time and source) and `ai_player` (the PlayerAi fields at `0x67d068` that Koprulu models). In a campaign game `SetupVictoryConditions 0x448530` marks every player's AI as campaign AI (flag `0x20`). The original keeps 2500 AI region records per player (`0x4aa310`) and 2500 reach marks (`0x6b1aa0`), and `FinishAiMapSetup 0x48dd80` copies the map's count of records out of them; Koprulu's arrays have the same size and a map of more regions fails at its start.

**The damage table.** `tools/research/probe_damage_table_117.py` maps the executable into Unicorn, replaces its writable data section with a state dump's (the DAT columns and upgrade levels as the game loaded them) and runs `WeaponDamage 0x41dfc0` for each weapon's base damage at a target CUnit of each type (player 0, incomplete, hit points 0x40000000, full shields), with the owner's upgrades at levels 0 and 3; the shield hit image is skipped. `tests/lua/test_damage_table.lua` retypes one unit of a native game the same way.

**Design correction (2026-10-14, nuke).** A Nuclear Missile exists only after its Silo trains it: the paid production of secondary order Train 38 and the Silo's order 124 (`0x41b4b0`, `0x4356c0`; docs/research/native-nuke.md), and a campaign map places no armed Silo (its UNIT relation flags link add-ons and Nydus Canals only). The Nuke (orders 123–129, `0x436540`, `0x4366f0`, `0x41eba0`, `0x41ea40`, `0x436720`) therefore moves to plan phase 6 with the production that makes its missile; its weapon effect (hit type 5) is ported with the others.

**Design correction (2026-10-14, repair).** The SCV's Repair (order 34, `0x41ad30`) pays every instalment from its owner's minerals and gas (`0x41b910`, `0x41bab0`); the players' stocks and their accounting come with the economy (plan phase 6), so Repair moves there with them. Recharging shields from a Shield Battery costs only its energy and stays in phase 4.

**Fields left out.** From the third step on, the unit's minimap blink counter (`+0x8a`, set to 20 when the local player's unit is attacked) is left out on both sides: the minimap's drawing counts it down (`DrawLocalMinimapUnits 0x46e490`), as the drawing refreshes the image bounds. So are its color (`+0x8b`) and the silence of the notices (`+0x87`): for a unit off the screen (`0x422c20`, the camera) the notice recolors the blink for the talking portrait (`0x4a7200`) and silences the notices within 352 pixels (`0x4460d0`), which the idle loop counts down every 100 ms or more (`0x446ff0`); nothing of the simulation reads either.

**Order dispatch and commands.** The dispatcher switches on the unit's current order at each stage (`0x439420`): an every-step handler that ends its order (MedicHealToIdle) lets the next order's periodic handler run in the same step. The game RNG runs while the turn's commands execute (`ExecuteGameTurn 0x4768a0`): a command that stops an attack plays an animation whose `waitrand` draws from it.

**Emulator runs.** A scenario run deletes the state dumps of an earlier, longer run before it dumps its own steps.

**The AI's resource areas.** GenerateResourceAreas `0x497a20` and RefreshResourceAreas `0x496c80` change only the area table and the area tags of the resources (+0xd8), which the state model leaves out, and draw no random number; nothing ported reads them. Koprulu does not build them yet (plan phase 7B, with the towns and expansions that read them), so a map with resources runs and their readers will fail until then.

**The screen's draws.** The original keeps the terrain the viewer remembers (the displayed megatile, the creep-shown flag `0x40000000`, the creep borders) and refreshes it where the viewer sees a change: every 100 steps off screen (`0x40af30`) and at each draw of the game screen for its 21x14 tiles (`0x40b220` with `0x40b310`). The emulator logs every on-screen refresh with the screen's top-left tile (`ui.jsonl` "onscreen"), and Koprulu replays it (`kp_refresh_terrain_onscreen`, 21x14 tiles); the viewer calls it with its camera's tiles at each draw, and the off-screen refresh leaves the last screen's tiles out. The dump of the receding buckets now reads their nine WORD counts (it read nine bytes; equal while the pool was unused).

**The hit history.** The Lurker line's hit history (32 slots of 16 source and target pairs, advanced each step by `AdvanceHitHistorySlot 0x40a880`) is kept in the save but not in the state dump; the scenarios check it through the damage it allows. TODO: dump it when a scenario needs it compared directly.

**Local selections in the emulator's log.** `ui.jsonl` records the interface's selections at `0x49cab0`, but not the game's own removals of a unit that dies or leaves sight (`0x4268d0`, `0x4a7b80` through `0x49cf80`): Koprulu makes those itself, and replaying them again would reselect twice.

**Scenario units.** A spec unit may give `"hp"`, `"shields"` and `"energy"` in percent (the CHK UNIT record's valid properties 0x2, 0x4, 0x8). `"hangar": n` fills a Carrier's or Reaver's hangar (valid property 0x20). An input `{"frame": F, "alliance": [a, b, v]}` sets player a's alliance towards b in both games (the emulator writes the table `0x534914` and logs it in `ui.jsonl`, Koprulu applies it with `kp_set_alliance`): a fixture of what a trigger's Set Alliance Status does before the triggers of plan phase 5. `"related": k` links a unit to spec unit k: a Nydus Canal to its exit, an add-on to its building (the CHK relation flags 0x200 and 0x400 and the related serial). `"researched": {"slot": [tech, ...]}` makes techs available and researched for a slot (the CHK sections PTEC and PTEx).

**Query frames.** The original's rectangle queries stack their results (`0x5d88f8`) so that a query nested in the walk of another keeps its results; the AI's reactions nest several. `kp_query_rect` keeps the same frames in the game (`KpSpatial.frames`) instead of a unit array on the C stack per level.

## Acceptance

**`bench_combat` on the Fold7** (2026-10-14, `tools/native/android_bench.sh bench_combat 10`): `kp_replay` equals the original at all 901 steps on the phone; `kp_bench` over 10 runs: mean 0.089 ms, median 0.055 ms, p99 0.45 ms, max 2.7 ms (plan §10: 4 ms, none above 8 ms). The first runs showed one pause of 120–210 ms per run at a random step, in `bench_200` too: the phone was dozing with its screen off, and `getrusage` showed the step's CPU time under 1 ms with one voluntary context switch, so the process was suspended, not computing. The script now refuses to run unless the phone is awake.

**Combat in the viewer** (2026-10-14): play check 3 (`viewer/play_combat.settings`, [viewer README](../viewer/README.md)) boxes twelve units of the human player's front in `bench_combat` and sends them into the computer's half; after 600 steps on macOS (the engine with the native extensions) and on HTML5 alike the human player keeps 53 of 100 units and the computer 76 of 100, with no error, and `tools/ci.sh` checks it on HTML5. A spike settings file outside the repository is refused now (`tools/spike.sh`): bob uploaded an old copy of the repository found next to such a file in a scratch directory, and the build server built that copy's native core.

**The final code** (commit 7e022eb): every scenario equals the original at every step in `tools/ci.sh`, with ASan and UBSan; on the Fold7 (2026-10-14, same command) `kp_replay` equals the original at all 901 steps and `kp_bench` over 10 runs gives mean 0.086 ms, median 0.053 ms, p99 0.44 ms, max 2.9 ms (step 49, once); play check 3 again gives 53 and 76 of 100 units on macOS (and on HTML5 in `tools/ci.sh`).
