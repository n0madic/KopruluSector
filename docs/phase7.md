# Phase 7 progress

Plan: phase 7 (the AIScript VM and the AI runtime, milestones 7A–7E). Started 2026-10-18, after the user approved the design (inline design document, 2026-10-18):
- the new records live in `KpGame` the way `KpGuards` does: fixed-size arrays, the original's pointers kept as index + 1 (or as a tagged reference `KP_AI_REF(kind, index)` in a unit's AI word: 1 guard, 2 worker, 3 building, 4 military; a training context `KP_AI_CONTEXT(kind, k)`: 1 region, 2 guard, 3 town), lists in the original's order: the script pool `KpScripts` (100 slots, `0x6a1a18`), the towns `KpTowns` (100, `0x690160`, with their 100 standing requests each), the worker, building and military records (1000 each, `0x67f7b0`, `0x685578`, `0x6acc50`), the resource areas `KpAreas` (250, `0x6a2e88`) and the PlayerAi records grown to the spending heap, the attack roster, the defense lists, the unit build limits and the medic list; the save format stays version 1 and grows with sections `SCRP`, `TOWN`, `WKAI`, `BDAI`, `MLAI`, `AREA` (the scripts' shared return PC `0x6a2e7c` saved explicitly);
- the native core loads `aiscript.kpd` (sections `AISC` and `BWSC`);
- the frame stays strictly sequential: the scheduler `0x4938e0` runs in `kp_ai_step` before the regional sweep, the spending pass `0x483400`, the towns' upkeep `0x486c80` and the attack evaluation in the step's AI timers, the worker, building and military services from ComputerAI 156;
- a branch without a port fails with its address (`kp_game_fail`), so the comparison with the emulator stops at the first missing mechanism; the melee start `StartCustomLevelAIScripts 0x4486a0` and the non-campaign planner stay outside V1;
- the oracle is the full-game emulator: new dump categories on both sides and an AI trace next to the trigger trace; the acceptance (user's choice) is all 64 campaign missions equal at every 20th step of 1–2001 with both traces, and long runs of about 10,000 steps of `terran05`, `zerg01`, `zerg02`, `protoss01`, `protoss02` and one Brood War mission of each race.

**Design corrections (2026-10-18).**
- The early unload of a computer's transport (`0x437902`) is refused with a worker aboard: the test reads the cargo unit's own flags (the port read the flags of its type's index as a unit before).
- Of the opcodes without a port, only send_suicide (Suic, SuiR) and junkyard_dog (JYDg) appear in the campaigns' scripts (`docs/research/evidence/ai-inventory.json`); the others (race_jump, region_size, get_oldpeons, groundmap_jump, guard_resources, wait_upgrades, wait_secure, build/wait_bunkers, build/wait_turrets, harass_factor, eval_harass, rush, implode, enter/exit_transport, make_patrol, debug, fatal_error) are melee only and still fail with their address, as do the non-campaign planner (`0x4aa350`, `0x4979b0`, `0x4b0be0`) and the melee start `0x4486a0`.
- The panic block's pseudo-script has flags 0 (`0x494c60`): its script runs from the original binary.
- IsRouteInteriorSafe `0x496ac0` (expansions, help requests) passes a route between groups and a route that the search does not find.
- The test suite got faster without checking less (2026-10-18): the native dump formats its numbers without printf, reuses a category's field order, streams each category's CRC, and CRC-32 runs by slicing-by-8 (the same bytes and CRCs; 23.9 → 5.5 ms per dump of `xterran-m46` at step 2001); `tests/lua/run.lua -j N` runs the suites in parallel with the long tests in shards (LuaJIT about 30 s instead of about 4 minutes, PUC Lua 5.1 1 minute instead of 4.5).
- Review fixes (2026-10-18): a computer's new unit of a town type (units.dat AI byte bit `0x2`) made by a trigger, a script, a gift or a rescue joins its owner's first town (AssignCreatedUnitAi `0x4b1fe0` → `0x484f40`; the port left it without AI once towns existed); where the original would hang or read past its tables the port fails with the address instead: a defense recipe of zero-strength types that never meets a region's need (`0x4af930`'s loop), a spending heap popped empty (`0x482310`), shared vision with a player byte past 11 (`0x4b0fe0`), a directory lookup in a binary shorter than one record (`0x494d20`); the AI modules name the original's identifiers once (`kp_ids.h`: unit types, orders, region states and flags, PlayerAi flags, controllers) and share the pools' list operations (`kp_pool_take`, `kp_pool_release`); the AI searches' scratch arrays live in the game (`KpAiScratch`) instead of in statics; the parallel Lua runner deletes the jobs' old logs before a run. The AI placement search was measured before any caching: 0–1 searches in 10,000 idle steps of the long runs, at most 0.54 ms each; a cache by region pair would change results, since SearchRegionPath `0x491300` measures from the exact points.
- Second review fixes (2026-10-18): CRC-32 uses constant tables generated by `tools/native/crc32_table.py` (checked by `tools/ci.sh`) instead of a lazy initialization that games on several threads could race on; the AI route cuts keep their copy of the region graph with the graph (`KpRegionGraph.saved_*`) and restore it also when a search fails; the dump's hex formatting falls back to printf past 16 digits and the guard-time walk is bounded like the other lists; `kp_ids.h` also names the units.dat AI flags, Ventral Sacs, MoveUnload and the requirement errors, and the spending kinds, the roster's size and lost entry are shared in `kp_game.h` (the remaining local copies in the AI, order, transport and step modules removed); the expansion choice reuses `kp_ai_has_transports`, `prep_down` the merged tank count, and every waiting opcode retries through one helper.
- Identifier cleanup (2026-10-18): all of kp_core names the original's identifiers once. `kp_ids.h` holds the unit types (with the mineral, beacon and trap ranges and `KP_TYPE_NONE`), the orders, techs, upgrades and weapons (openbw's names where the port had none, with their `NONE` sentinels), the controllers, races and the neutral player; `kp_images_internal.h` holds the iscript animations. 915 local `#define`s in 62 modules are gone. Several local names did not match their values and are now named after them (the values were right, checked against the atlas): order 34 Repair (`ORDER_HARVEST_GAS` in TryAiTransportForMove `0x437540`), 119 CastDarkSwarm (`ORDER_CONSUME`, `0x49cc40`), 112 MoveUnload (`ORDER_PICKUP`, `ORDER_RETREAT`), 111 Unload (`ORDER_RETREAT_FROM`), 122 CastEMPShockwave (`ORDER_CAST_DETECTOR`, `0x4ae3e0`), 31 PlaceProtossBuilding (`ORDER_CONSTRUCTING` in the owner and power modules), controller 4 rescue active (`CONTROLLER_NEUTRAL` in OrderFollow `0x428310`), types 35 and 36 Larva and Egg (`INFESTED_TERRAN_A/B` in `0x414080`), and `kp_ids.h`'s own Sieging 98 and Unsieging 99 were swapped. The compiled objects are byte-identical before and after.
- Identifier literals (2026-10-18): the bare numbers that were ids of the original's tables are named too, 458 `kp_ids.h` names now (108 new): comparisons, assignments, `case` labels and returns on unit types, orders, techs, upgrades, weapons, controllers and races, the arguments of functions that take one (`kp_upgrade_level`, `kp_validate_spell_target`, `attempt`/`ready` of the AI spells), the dispatch tables of `kp_order_dispatch.c` (the order of every entry), the recipe tables of `kp_owner.c`, the AI region states in `kp_ai.c`, and the requirement errors 11 (`KP_ERR_BURROWED`) and 15 (`KP_ERR_RANGE_ORDER`). A bare variable counts only where it is known to be a type (`t` is also a unit index in many functions), and a literal 0 is a sentinel, not Marine or Die, unless it meets a unit's own field. Sentinels meet the `NONE` names (`0x82` weapon, `0x2c` tech, 228 type). The compiled objects are again byte-identical. Not named: the lists and ranges that are not table ids (command ids, the right-click action, sound and image ids, sprite ids of the doodads), and the flags of units.dat.
- The stalled defense recipe is unreachable with the stock scripts (2026-11-02). The original hangs in `0x4af930`'s loop and kp stops at `recipe_stalled`; both need a defense opcode that names a type without strength in its list's credited direction. `tools/research/inspect_ai_defense_strength.py` checks every instruction of `aiscript.bin` and `bwscript.bin` against the strengths of the stock `units.dat` and `weapons.dat`. None of the 2,568 defense opcodes does so; the weakest entries are the Marine at 100 (gg), the Scourge at 70 (ag, aa) and the Wraith at 130 (ga) ([defense recipes](research/native-defense-recipes.md#the-stock-scripts-name-no-zero-strength-type), `docs/research/evidence/ai-defense-strength.json`). The failure stays as the original's address rather than invented progress. `test_aiscript.stock_defense_types_have_strength` keeps kp's strength table to the evidence. `inspect_ai.py` now exposes `load_schema` and `decode`; its output is byte-identical.
